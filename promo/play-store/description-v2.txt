Off Carrom is a free arcade carrom board game with a warm wooden look, built for quick matches and a long career. Flick the striker, pocket your coins and cover the queen in this offline carrom pool game.

Slide your striker along the baseline, aim, set the power and flick. Play Classic, Freestyle and fast special rules like Rapid Fire, Knockout, Queen's Gambit, Target Practice, Bank Shots, Sprint and Combo Chain.

CARROM GAME MODES
- Career: play through 10 venues, each with its own boss and four games in different rule sets. Win to unlock the next venue and earn power-ups.
- Quick Match: pick a board, rule set and opponent and play right away.
- Tournament: beat every rival in a knockout bracket to win the trophy.
- Shop: spend your winnings on strikers, grips and powder with real stat bonuses.
- Tutorial and hints: learn aiming, power and bank shots, with hints you can switch on or off in Options.
